# GR1 Madeira Crossing — tent-free plan

Trip context: Madeira **6–19 September 2026**. Full crossing ~**95 km**, typically **5–6 walking days**.

## Tent or no tent?

| Mode | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| **Without tent** | Yes — hotels/guesthouses + Achada lodge; 1–2 taxi links (see stages below). |
| **With tent** | **Yes, legally — only at designated IFCN campsites**, with a **SIMplifica camping licence** (€5 / tent / night). **Wild camping is illegal** and policed. |

Camping is often the better fit for a continuous feel: official sites sit on/near the GR1 corridor (Ribeiro Frio, Pico Ruivo, Bica da Cana, Fanal, etc.). Capacité is tiny on key nights — book early.

### Rules (IFCN)

- Book **Licença para Campismo** on [SIMplifica](https://simplifica.madeira.gov.pt/services/8-25-101) **before** each night.
- Carry the permit; Forest Police can check.
- No fires except designated spots; pack out all rubbish; 5 m between tents (except same household).
- Fee: **€5/tent/night** (RAM residents free with card).

### Tent itinerary (east → west, ~6 nights)

Same date window: **finish before PR1 closes 14–17 Sep**.

| Night | Campsite | After stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Chão das Feiteiras or Boca das Voltas / Terreiros | Coastal / Portela approach |
| 2 | Ribeiro Frio **or** Poiso | Into the mountains |
| 3 | **Pico Ruivo** (2 pitches — book first) | After PR1 Areeiro → Ruivo |
| 4 | Chão dos Louros **or** Bica da Cana / Estanquinhos | West of Encumeada / plateau — **taxi or detour** while PR1.3 Torrinhas→Encumeada is closed |
| 5 | Ribeira do Alecrim **or** Fanal | 25 Fontes / Fanal corridor |
| 6 | Fanal (if not night 5) or finish Porto Moniz hotel | Optional last camp before coast |

**PR1.3 gap still applies with a tent:** you cannot legally/continuous-walk Ruivo → Encumeada until that section reopens. Common fix: camp Pico Ruivo → descend PR1.2 or go Torrinhas→Curral, then reconnect to Chão dos Louros / Bica da Cana (taxi or road walk as needed).

Trail **SIMplifica** slots (PR1, PR6, …) are **separate** from camping licences — book both.

### Pico Ruivo exit (current rules)

- **PR1.2** Achada do Teixeira — easiest for tent-free lodging (Mountain Spot).
- **PR1.3** only open **Ruivo → Boca das Torrinhas** (~3.9 km); then **PR2** to Curral das Freiras (lodging + bus/taxi). Torrinhas → Encumeada **still closed**.
- Do **not** plan continuous footpath Ruivo → Encumeada until IFCN reopens that section.

## Bookings (order)

1. **SIMplifica** — PR1 early slot on Day 3 (before 14 Sep).
2. Ribeiro Frio + Achada / Ruivo night.
3. Encumeada + Seixal + Porto Moniz.
4. Other IFCN overlaps (PR6, PR13 / Fanal corridor, etc.).
5. Taxi Achada → Encumeada for Day 4 morning.

Fees (non-residents): most trails **€4.50**; full **PR1 €10.50**; multi-trail day / 3-day / 7-day rates may be cheaper if many PR sections.

## Gear (tent-free)

Daypack-friendly: trail shoes, rain shell, warm layer (ridge wind), headlamp (tunnels), 2–3 L water capacity, offline GPX, SIMplifica QR offline. Laundry possible in Seixal/hotels — no need to carry a full week of clothes.
